Nodus Reader 0.1.0 (49)

- Select a name in a book or PDF Intelligent Reading and choose Pronunciation. Enter how it should sound, compare Original and With correction, and save it for the entire book.
- Open Pronunciations from the book or player menu to add, edit and delete rules. Check compound names, accents and capitalisation; partial matches inside longer words should remain unchanged.
- Listen with Kokoro or Phonon, including sentences already generated before the correction. On Mac, also try Ming. Only affected sentences should need new audio.
- Check that the original name stays visible and highlighted even when its pronunciation uses several words. Starting from a word and resuming after a pause should preserve the correct text position.
- With optional iCloud sync enabled on both devices, check that additions, edits and deletions to the book’s dictionary arrive on the other device. Voice models and generated audio remain local.

Pronunciation spelling is interpreted by the chosen voice and language; preview it again after changing voices.
