Private build 71. Neural narration no longer pauses every few sentences: saving the reading position (every sentence) made the app throw away the sentences it had already prepared, so the voice ran out of them. Measured on an iPhone 16 Pro Max with Phonon: from 17 pauses in five minutes to none, keeping 35-50 s of voice ready ahead, also while soundscapes are composed. Composing now gives way to the voice whenever its cushion runs low. Pauses in the middle of sentences are gone too: abbreviations (Mr., Dr., Sr., D., p. ej.), initials (J. R. R. Tolkien) and dialogue tags ("Is it you?" he asked) no longer split a sentence. While narration plays, the position is still saved on the device every sentence but uploaded to iCloud at most once a minute; pausing, stopping or leaving the app uploads it at once.
