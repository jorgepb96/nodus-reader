New since build 73.

- Voice volume: the reader's atmosphere panel has a voice control beside Music and Ambience; it starts at 100% and you can lower it under the music.
- Voice filters adapt to each other: with a model chosen, only its languages and genders are listed, and the other way round. The dropdowns no longer have a search field.
- Deleting a book asks beside that book, not at the bottom or top of the screen.
- Settings › Soundscapes shows the AI models as installed as soon as the download finishes.
- On iPad, the original page beside PDF Intelligent Reading follows the reader's theme and hides or shows with the button beside the mode picker.
- Tested on a real iPhone 16 Pro Max, including the Share Extension from Safari.

AI soundscapes
Settings › Soundscapes: turn them on, choose AI and download Music and Ambience in Models (about 2.6 GB, on Wi-Fi); then press play in a book and choose how many pieces to compose first. Powered by Stability AI; everything runs on your iPhone. Tell us how they sound, waiting times, heat and battery.
