One window for everything. Settings now opens in the main window beside the sidebar (the gear at the bottom of the sidebar, or ⌘,). Books open in the same window: show the library sidebar while reading with the sidebar button next to Back, or ⌃⌘S. New Studio tab in Voices & Models: record your voice or add an audio file (WAV, MP3, M4A, AIFF, FLAC or CAF) for Ming 3.0, rename it or delete it; Studio voices appear with Ming's voices. Playing a voice sample shows a spinner while the voice loads. The sidebar and the main area now share the same colours, and the Import button no longer shows an extra bubble.
