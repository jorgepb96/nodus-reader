iCloud synchronization now follows your library changes: saved reading and listening positions, bookmarks, notes, book imports, collections and deletions.

Changes are kept in a persistent queue, grouped by item and sent without overlapping sync passes. Failed operations retry automatically, including after connection recovery. Edits made during an upload remain queued for the next pass.

CloudKit failures are checked per record, concurrent changes use conflict protection, and pending file transfers or cleanup are no longer silently discarded. Cover replacements and cloud-only books are handled more reliably. Sync status now distinguishes pending changes from a completed sync.

Changes from other devices are checked when opening or returning to the app and every minute while it is active. Books and narration continue to work offline.
