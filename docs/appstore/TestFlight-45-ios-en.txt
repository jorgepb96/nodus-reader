New: Immersive Audio (Settings › Immersive Audio, off until you turn it on). Quiet instrumental music and ambient sound while you read or listen. Download a sound pack first (Essentials is about 35 MB); the sounds then play offline. Automatic mode follows the story with Apple Intelligence on this device and changes only at clear new scenes; Study keeps one steady atmosphere; Manual lets you pick rain, forest, fireplace, library and more. Music softens while narration speaks. Please try it with headphones and tell us if anything sounds too loud, changes too often or interrupts the narration.
