Smoother typing, selecting and playback.

- Search fields in dropdowns (voice filters, the book's voice picker, Voices & Models, the languages list, the iPad sidebar) show each letter at once. The list filters when you pause typing.
- Press and drag to select text while narration plays. The selection no longer gets lost, and the text stays still while your finger is on it or a passage is selected. Following resumes when you let go.
- The player, the reader's menus and sheets no longer stutter during narration.
- While playing, the reading position is saved every 15 seconds (pausing or closing the book still saves at once).
- While an AI soundscape is being composed, touching the screen gives the interface priority for a moment.
