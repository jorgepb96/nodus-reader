Private build 68. With AI soundscapes, starting to listen (from the reader, the player or Home) first asks how many pieces to compose for the session: 1, 3 (recommended) or 6. A progress bar shows while they are composed and the voice starts when they are ready, so the two never compete; more pieces follow later as the story needs them. Start without waiting skips it; Don't ask for this book turns it off for that book (back on from the book's atmosphere panel); Don't ask again turns it off everywhere (back on in Settings › Soundscapes › More options). Scrolling stays smooth while a soundscape is being composed: composing gives the graphics processor back while you scroll.
