Private build 64. Fixes the crash when leaving the app while a soundscape was being composed: composing now stops as soon as the app stops being active, and an error from the music engine no longer closes the app. The first composed piece plays at once while nothing is playing, until one for the scene's own mood is ready. Pieces you ask for (Prepare, or an ambience or mood you choose) are composed in Low Power Mode too; only the pieces planned ahead wait for it to be off. In Low Power Mode a neural voice that falls behind waits a moment to play two sentences in a row instead of stopping after each one, and the player explains why it may pause. The player now always credits the voice model, "Powered by" its name and licence, each linked to the model page and the licence text.
