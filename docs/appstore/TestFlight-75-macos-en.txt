Nodus Reader for Mac, build 75.

AI soundscapes
Settings › Soundscapes: turn them on, choose AI and download Music and Ambience in Models (about 2.9 GB). Music and ambiences are composed on this Mac with Stable Audio 3 Small (Powered by Stability AI), follow the book's style and scenes, are kept to play offline, and nothing leaves the device. When you press play, the app asks how many pieces to compose before the voice.

Reader and narration
- Voice volume control in the atmosphere panel, beside Music and Ambience.
- Neural narration no longer pauses between sentences, and the highlight covers every line of the narrated sentence.
- The original page beside PDF Intelligent Reading follows the reader's theme and hides or shows with its button or Option-Command-I.
- The voice filters adapt to each other; Ming, Studio voices and Qwen remain available on the Mac.
