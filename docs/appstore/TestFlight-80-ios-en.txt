Nodus Reader 1.0.0 (80): CarPlay, and a choice of narration pace.

CarPlay
- Listen in the car. Nodus Reader now appears in CarPlay with two tabs, Recent and All Books. Choose a book and its narration continues where you left it. The Now Playing screen shows the chapter and the cover, a Chapters button to jump to another chapter, and a speed button. Book text is never shown in the car.

Read & Listen
- Narration pace, in Settings › Voices & Models. Natural, the default, keeps the pauses each voice leaves between sentences. Brisk shortens them by punctuation and reads very short sentences together with the next one.

Everything from build 79 is included.
