Nodus Reader 0.1.0 (56) — Private Mac beta

• Narration in Now Playing, with title, author, cover, system playback controls and media keys for play, pause and sentence navigation.
• Click a word to start narration there, preserving drag selection and double-click behavior. Arrow keys navigate or scroll; paged reading also has side navigation buttons.
• Sidebar toggle precedes Back. Library headers share a row, with a consistent top background and more space below Nodus / Reader.
• Library table columns fit the available width and populated metadata. The bottom player reserves its own space so content buttons remain accessible.
• Compact expanded player in two columns, sized to the window. Clicking outside or Escape closes it without stopping audio. Check atmosphere, cache and voice controls.
• Multi-select model, language and gender voice filters persist across books and sessions. Reading-flow controls no longer show a duplicate label and are unavailable while narration auto-scroll is enabled.
• Check highlighting with light/dark themes, EPUB/PDF/text, engine switching and position retention. Qwen remains experimental on Mac; also test Linnet, Kokoro, Phonon and Ming.

macOS distribution is private only. The public Mac release is coming later. Please report the chip, OS version, model, voice and steps for any issue.
