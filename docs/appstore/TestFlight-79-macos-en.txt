Nodus Reader for Mac 1.0.0 (79): an idea from TestFlight feedback.

Read & Listen
- Hear a voice before choosing it. In the player's "Voice for this book" list, each voice now has a play button on the right. It spins while the voice loads, plays a short sample, and stops if you click it again or close the list. Clicking the voice's name still chooses it for the book.

Everything from build 78 is included.
