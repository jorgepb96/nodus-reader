Nodus Reader 0.1.0 (48)

- Check automatic Read & Listen scrolling in PDF Intelligent Reading: the text should follow the spoken line without jumping up and down.
- When narration finishes, tap the circular replay arrow to listen again from the beginning of the document. Pausing should still resume where you stopped.
- Home now groups documents into EPUB, Texts, PDF and other formats. Empty formats are hidden and appear when documents are added. See all opens the chosen format.
- Pasted and imported TXT documents share the Texts shelf, including finished documents.
- Settings opens on the App theme instead of the Reader theme, including when you return to Settings.
- Open an iCloud-only document and check that the download completes before reading starts.

Voice models and generated audio remain on your device. iCloud sync is optional.
