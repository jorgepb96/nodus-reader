Nodus Reader 0.1.0 (55) — What to test

VOICE SWITCHING AND RESPONSIVENESS
• In EPUB, PDF and plain text, select Qwen and switch back to a faster model while it loads or generates a sentence. Try both the book voice picker and Settings. Check that the interface remains responsive, preserves your position, and does not restore a cancelled request's voice or loading indicator.
• Switch rapidly between voice previews, then return to narration. The previous preview should stop without affecting its replacement.
• Model loading and sentence generation now use different messages. “Generating the next sentence…” means audio is being synthesized, not that the reference voice is being prepared again.

QWEN AND OFFLINE AUDIO
• Takes that reach a generation limit remain rejected even if trailing silence can be trimmed. Check recovery after a failed sentence: up to three takes, a pause at the same position, and the ability to retry or choose another voice.
• Older Qwen audio is regenerated under a new cache revision. Saved offline jobs migrate their keys and recalculate progress while preserving an explicit pause. Include pronunciation rules in testing.
• Qwen remains experimental. This build does not claim faster sustained synthesis or change its sampling or weights. On iPhone generation may fall behind playback; generating offline audio first is recommended.

REGRESSIONS
• Test Linnet, Kokoro and Phonon, word highlighting, pause/resume, speed changes, headphones and locked-screen playback. Limit each continuous phone session to five minutes.
• Report device, model, voice, document format, and whether the issue occurred during loading, generation or playback. Full physical-device confirmation of model switching and the reported app termination remains pending; this beta provides the changes to validate.
