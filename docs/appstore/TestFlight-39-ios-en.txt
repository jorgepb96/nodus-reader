New: recaps and characters with Apple Intelligence (supported iPhone and iPad, iOS 26 or later). In the reader, the open-book button shows "Where you left off", "The story so far" and the characters you have met, written on the device from the pages you have already read, so nothing ahead is given away. Coming back to a book after two days or more shows "Welcome back" with the recap. Turn it off in Settings › Recaps & Characters. The text is generated and can contain mistakes: tell us how it works with your books, especially long ones and from different positions. Kokoro now uses a newer MLX.
