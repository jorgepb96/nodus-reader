Nodus Reader 1.0.0 — notes for App Review

No account is needed and nothing is required to sign in. iCloud sync is optional and off by default.

Getting started
1. On first launch choose "Use iOS voices" (listens immediately, no download), "Download a model" or "Just read".
2. Import any DRM-free EPUB, PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML or RTF with the + button (Files), or share a web page to Nodus Reader from Safari. Free public-domain books are available from Project Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org). DRM-protected books are not supported.
3. Open the book and tap Listen (or tap any word) to hear it read aloud with the text highlighted.

Optional downloads (all on request, nothing is bundled)
- Natural voices: Settings › Voices & Models › Models. Kokoro and Phonon download from Hugging Face. Two Phonon models (Spanish and Multilingual) are licensed for non-commercial use by their publisher and require the user's own Hugging Face account; signing in happens on Hugging Face's page and is never needed for the rest of the app.
- Soundscapes (Settings › Soundscapes, off by default): downloaded sound packs (CC0/CC-BY, credited in the app) or AI soundscapes. AI soundscapes need an iPhone or iPad with Apple silicon and a download of about 2.6–2.9 GB; music and ambience are then composed on the device with Stable Audio 3 Small ("Powered by Stability AI", Community License registered). No book text or audio leaves the device.

On-device processing
Narration, PDF text recognition and reading, recaps and character lists (Apple Intelligence, on-device model only, never Private Cloud Compute) and soundscape composition all run on the device. The app has no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no Nodus account. Privacy policy: https://nodusresearch.com/reader/privacy/

Permissions
The microphone permission string exists for creating custom voices, which is a Mac-only feature; the iPhone and iPad app never asks for it. Background audio is used for narration and lock-screen controls.
