Private build 70. AI soundscapes tuned on real iPhones (16 Pro Max and iPhone 13). The first music of a session is ready in about 6 s on an iPhone 16 Pro Max and about 11 s on an iPhone 13 (it was about 18 s and up to 97 s): the composer warms up while you choose how many pieces, the first piece is a 30 s one, and pieces you asked for follow without long pauses. A full session of three (two music pieces and an ambience) takes about 27 s on an iPhone 16 Pro Max. Ambiences such as rain are no longer rejected for coming in gusts, a piece that fails its loop is retried once, and a bug that could break loop repair is fixed. On iPhones with 4 GB, memory warnings no longer stop composing over and over, and if the iPhone gets warm the voice starts with the first piece ready while the rest follow. Downloading a second model while another downloads now queues it instead of greying out the button.
